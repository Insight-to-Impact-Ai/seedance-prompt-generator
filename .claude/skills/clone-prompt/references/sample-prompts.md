# Sample prompts

Six complete prompts across the families. Plain text inside the fences is exactly what gets pasted.


### Locked studio talking head (dialogue, oner)

```
FORMAT: 13s / single continuous shot / studio talking head — the creator reflecting on AI clone tech

SUBJECT: @image_1.

WARDROBE: As shown in @image_1.

ENVIRONMENT: Same as @image_1.

STYLE ANCHOR: Locked studio talking head, podcast / YouTube creator aesthetic. Cinematic prosumer camera (Sony A7S3-style), locked tripod, no camera movement. Medium close-up framing — head, shoulders, and the podcast mic in the lower foreground. Slight shallow DOF on background only — face fully sharp, background warmly blurred. Warm key light from camera-front, gentle fill, soft warm rim from the right. Photorealistic — natural human skin of @image_1 with visible pores, freckles, real eye moisture, subtle natural micro-movements.

DELIVERY: Lip-sync driven by @audio_1.

LOGIC RULE: Single continuous shot. No cuts, no jumps, no zoom — locked tripod throughout. Subject remains seated, framing stays fixed. Natural head movement and hand gestures evolve organically with the speech, no static movement, no looping. The podcast mic stays in the lower foreground throughout.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: No music, no captions.

---

ACTION:

0:00 to 0:13 — Locked studio MCU, podcast talking head. One continuous take.

@image_1 sits at his desk behind a podcast mic, looking directly into the lens with relaxed engagement and a slight forward lean. He says with a small head tilt, "Yeah that's scary right?"

He continues into the body of the thought, head moving naturally with the rhythm of the speech: "Like this technology has come a far way, because I used to have an ai clone and everyone called me out on it. They all noticed it was fake."

A brief pause. He sighs softly and leans forward a touch, a slight knowing half-smile forming. Then, slower now, he says, "But if you can't tell, this is AI too."

He holds the lens for a beat, the half-smile lingering.

Lip-sync driven by @audio_1.
```

### Vlog selfie outdoor with placement (found-footage, oner)

```
FORMAT: 15s / single continuous shot / vlog selfie walk-and-set-down about AI clones

SUBJECT: @image_1.

WARDROBE: As shown in @image_1 (cream cotton work jacket over white t-shirt). Black pants.

ENVIRONMENT: Outdoors, late afternoon city setting — a metal railing at chest height where he places the camera. After placement, the top edge of the railing is visible across the bottom of the frame. Real ambient sound — distant traffic, soft footsteps, gentle breeze.

STYLE ANCHOR: First-person POV, found-footage logic. The viewer's perspective IS the camera throughout — there is no camera as an object in the world of the scene. Wide-ish prosumer lens look, no fisheye. Walking phase: handheld, the frame sways naturally with his stride. Set-down phase: the frame lowers onto the railing, locks, stays static. After placement, the locked frame captures him in seated medium close-up. Full DOF — everything sharp, no cinematic blur. Photorealistic, natural human skin of @image_1 with visible pores, freckles, real eye moisture, subtle natural micro-movements.

DELIVERY: Lip-sync driven by @audio_1.

LOGIC RULE: Single continuous shot. No cuts, no jumps, no zoom. First-person POV throughout — the viewer's perspective IS the camera. There is no camera body, no recording device, and no equipment as an object anywhere in the scene. After the perspective comes to rest on the railing, the railing is empty — only the clean top edge visible at the bottom of frame, with nothing else on it.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: No music, no captions.

---

ACTION:

0:00 to 0:15 — First-person POV, handheld → locked on a railing. One continuous take.

Opens with @image_1 walking forward outdoors, a few people and cars in the background, the frame swaying naturally with his stride, his face filling the shot, alive vlogger energy in his eyes, eyebrows lifting as he says, "Okay, this is crazy."

He slows and the perspective itself lowers smoothly toward a flat grey railing at chest height, then settles and locks fully into place. The top edge of the empty railing is now visible across the bottom of the frame. He says, "Come a bit closer because we need to talk about AI clones," and bends forward into a tight medium close-up.

"What you're seeing right now is my very own clone."

He slowly turns his face to the left, holds, then to the right — deliberately showing his skin texture, freckles, and mustache to the camera. "This is not real, this is all AI. Just look how realistic the movements are."

His right hand enters frame in a sweeping open-palm gesture toward his own face.

Lip-sync driven by @audio_1.
```

### Static observational walk-past (locked-camera bug fix in action)

```
FORMAT: 5s / single continuous shot / STATIC LOCKED close-up side-profile walk-past

SUBJECT: @image_1.

WARDROBE: Cream pullover hoodie over a white t-shirt as shown in @image_1, plain black baseball cap, fitted black pants, sneakers.

PROPS: The Rimowa silver hard-shell suitcase as shown in @image_2 — polished aluminum with vertical ribbed grooves and the colorful travel stickers on the front.

ENVIRONMENT: Inside a massive airport departure terminal at golden hour, busy with travelers. Floor-to-ceiling glass wall running the length of the background, looking out onto the tarmac with passenger planes visible through the glass. Other travelers moving through the terminal behind him in the middle ground. Real busy terminal ambient sound. STRONG warm golden afternoon sunlight pouring through the glass wall, casting long warm light shapes across the terminal floor and bathing the entire scene in deep amber.

STYLE ANCHOR: Static locked tripod shot, documentary observation aesthetic. The camera is rigidly fixed on a tripod and does not move, pan, zoom, dolly, or track at any point during the entire shot. Close side-profile framing — camera positioned at chest-height perpendicular to his walking path, framing him from waist to head as he passes. The frame composition is fixed; he enters from one side, walks past the camera position, and exits the other side. STRONG golden-hour color palette — rich amber, orange, and honey tones, warm directional sunlight from the glass wall side. Ultra-detailed skin texture — visible pores, freckles, individual mustache hairs, real eye moisture, fabric weave on the hoodie. The realism IS the content. Photorealistic, no plastic skin.

DELIVERY: No dialogue.

LOGIC RULE: Single continuous shot. The camera is LOCKED — it does not move, pan, zoom, dolly, track, tilt, or follow the subject at any point. The frame composition is fixed throughout the entire 5 seconds. Subject enters the frame from the left, walks past the static camera position, and exits the frame on the right — the camera does NOT follow him. His walk is natural and grounded — relaxed gait, normal arm swing, weight shifting realistically, pulling the suitcase smoothly behind him. Lighting is strongly warm/golden — never cool, never neutral.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: No music, no captions, no camera movement, no cool lighting.

---

ACTION:

0:00 to 0:05 — Static locked tripod, side-profile close shot, camera at chest-height. Camera does not move.

@image_1 enters the frame from the left edge, walks across in close side profile, and exits the right edge — pulling the @image_2 Rimowa silver suitcase behind him. The camera stays completely fixed; the frame composition does not change. Behind him in the middle ground, other travelers move past in soft focus, and through the floor-to-ceiling glass wall a parked passenger plane and a taxiing one are visible on the tarmac. His walk is natural and grounded — relaxed gait, normal arm swing, weight shifting realistically. Pace is relaxed and purposeful, gaze ahead, not acknowledging the camera.
```

### Handheld vlog selfie with diegetic pan (window reveal)

```
FORMAT: 6s / single continuous shot / handheld vlog selfie on plane, pans to window

SUBJECT: @image_1.

WARDROBE: Cream pullover hoodie over a white t-shirt as shown in @image_1, plain black baseball cap.

ENVIRONMENT: Window seat on a passenger plane mid-flight. He's seated with the window to his right. Through the window: blue skies above a soft cloud layer below, golden light from the sun pouring in through the window. Warm interior plane lighting. Real ambient sound — soft engine hum, faint cabin chatter.

STYLE ANCHOR: Handheld vlog selfie aesthetic, found-footage logic. The frame the viewer sees through IS the camera he is holding in his hand — not a separate object. iPhone or small handheld camera look, no fisheye, no extreme distortion. Selfie framing initially — head and shoulders, the cabin around him visible. After 2 seconds, he physically pans the camera off himself toward the window to his right. Soft natural handheld sway throughout, slightly more movement during the pan. Full DOF — everything sharp. Photorealistic, natural human skin of @image_1 with visible pores, freckles, real eye moisture, subtle natural micro-movements. Soft golden light spilling in through the window.

DELIVERY: No dialogue.

LOGIC RULE: Single continuous shot. No cuts, no jumps. The camera is the handheld device he is holding — there is no separate, second, or duplicate camera or recording device visible at any point. The pan is physical — he turns his hand and the camera with it, panning from his face to the window across roughly 1 second of motion. The pan is the only camera movement; before and after the pan, the camera is held relatively steady with natural handheld sway only.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: No music, no captions.

---

ACTION:

0:00 to 0:06 — Handheld selfie camera, found-footage POV. One continuous take.

Opens with @image_1 already settled in his window seat on a plane, the handheld camera held at arm's length pointed at his face, soft golden window light catching the side of his face. He looks at the lens with relaxed energy and gives a soft small smile to the camera. After about two seconds, he physically pans the camera off his face and toward the window to his right, the pan revealing the blue sky and soft cloud layer outside, golden sunlight pouring through the window. The frame settles on the view.
```

### iPhone observer with smooth zoom (no dialogue, oner with deliberate zoom)

```
FORMAT: 10s / single continuous shot / iPhone footage of @image_1 at a casual meetup in a park

SUBJECT: @image_1.

WARDROBE: Denim button-down jacket over a white t-shirt as shown in @image_1.

ENVIRONMENT: Outdoor public park in bright daylight — grass, paths, trees. Background activity: skateboarders rolling past in the distance, people lounging on the grass, joggers, a dog walker. Real ambient park sounds.

STYLE ANCHOR: iPhone footage aesthetic, found-footage logic. The frame the viewer sees through IS the iPhone held by someone outside the gathering — not a separate object. iPhone wide lens look, no fisheye, no extreme distortion. Opens at a medium shot, then smooth digital zoom-in over the course of the shot pushes to a medium close-up on @image_1. Slight natural handheld sway throughout. Full DOF — everything sharp from foreground to background, no shallow depth-of-field, no blur. Phone-cam color science. Photorealistic, natural human skin of @image_1 with visible pores, freckles, real eye moisture.

DELIVERY: No specific dialogue audio. Natural park ambience.

LOGIC RULE: Single continuous shot. No cuts, no jumps. Smooth digital zoom-in is allowed — opens at medium, pushes in to medium close-up by the end. No panning. The iPhone is held by an observer outside the gathering; no other cameras visible. Casual meetup style — no podium, no lectern, no microphone stand, no stage, nothing in front of him. He stands freely with his hands free for gestures. Five to six real-looking people stand close to him in an informal circle, leaning in to hear, reacting naturally — they do not speak.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: No music, no captions, no podium.

---

ACTION:

0:00 to 0:10 — iPhone handheld shot in a park, observer outside the gathering, smooth digital zoom-in.

Opens at a medium shot — @image_1 standing on a path in a sunny public park at the center of a casual meetup, five to six people gathered close around him in an informal circle, leaning in to listen. Nothing in front of him — his hands are free. In the background, skateboarders roll past, people lounge on the grass. He is mid-conversation, speaking animatedly, his hands moving expressively.

The iPhone smoothly digital-zooms in over the course of the shot, pushing past the gathering's shoulders to land in a medium close-up on @image_1's face mid-gesture, his hands still entering the lower frame in conversational beats.
```
